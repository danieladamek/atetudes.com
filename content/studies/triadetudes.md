blurb: >-
  Triads, isolated. Pick a key, a scale and a string set, then run three-note voicings through a progression — cycling 4ths, 6ths, 3rds, or scalar — taking the smallest voice movement between them. An isolation box frames the region you're working in; click notes on the neck to write the figure, choose how it moves, and enclose a target tone when you want the approach. Chord, bass and click each get their own level, so muting the chords turns the page into a play-along you supply the voicings for. The practice log keeps what you worked on.
