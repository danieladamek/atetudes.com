  The At-Etudes metronome, standing alone. Three synthesized click voices — beep, wood, tick — with tap tempo, accents on the bar or the beat, time signatures through 7/4, and subdivisions to 16ths. Every At-Etudes app carries this same metronome as its first block, so the clock you learn here is the clock everywhere else. Beside it sits the notepad: type a plain line about what just happened, and Save files the idea with the tempo and settings it arrived at — Apply on any saved note puts the metronome back to that moment. Export writes one `.atchart.md` any sibling app can open. No audio assets, and it works offline from a double-click.
