Choose a key, a cycle, a string set and a voicing family — close, drop-2, drop-3 — and walk four-note chords through a progression with minimal movement between them. Arpeggiate the voicings or enclose a target tone, and watch it on the neck, the staff and the keyboard while you hear it on the metronome's grid, the bar split deciding how many beats each chord gets. Build the harmony up or break it down. Works offline, and the practice log is yours.
