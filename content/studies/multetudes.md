blurb: >-
  Build an étude from the ground up. Frame a position on the neck — drag the grip across frets and strings, drag the handles to choose which strings it spans — then pick what sits on it: a scale, a dyad, a triad, a tetrad or a shell. Write the figure in slots or in chord tones, say how it moves — blocked or arpeggiated, gripped or along a line — and run it through a progression built from a cycle, a form, or your own romans and bars. It plays on the neck, the staff and the keyboard at once, on the metronome's grid. Presets seed every control and leave them all live, so a starting point is never a lock, and notes and charts save to a file that never leaves your machine.
