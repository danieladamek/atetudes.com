title: Multetudes — the Étude Designer
layout: study
summary: "Build an étude from the ground up. Choose a key and a scale, frame a position on the neck, pick what sits on it — a scale, a dyad, a triad, a tetrad or a shell — and say how it moves: blocked or arpeggiated, gripped or along a line. Write the figure in slots or in chord tones, run it through a progression, and watch it on the neck, the staff and the keyboard while you hear it. Notes and charts save to a file that never leaves your machine. Works offline."
