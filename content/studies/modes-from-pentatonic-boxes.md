blurb: >-
  Every mode is a pentatonic scale you already know, plus two notes. Pick a key and the five pentatonic boxes appear in order down the neck, each showing the two mode tones that turn that box into the full mode. Toggle the mode tones out to strip back to the bare pentatonic, or flip any mode pair to its relative minor and watch the same physical notes change color and meaning. A drone sustains a tonal center — the whole map's, or one box's, following its flips — and the play buttons sound each box in turn. Click a color in the legend to pin that degree family across every box.
