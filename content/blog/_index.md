---
title: Slog
---

A slog is slow, heavy going: long hours of work against resistance. That is what practice is,
and this is where I keep mine in the open. It is a practice journal more than an informational
blog. I use it to capture my intentions, observations, challenges and discoveries in support
of my own musical growth. The prose is not meant to be pedagogical, and it may be poorly
structured, grammatically broken and stylistically bereft. Still, I hope anyone who finds a
post interesting enough to sit with it for a bit takes something away that contributes to
their own musical growth.
