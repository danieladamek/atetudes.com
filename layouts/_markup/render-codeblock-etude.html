{{- /* THE ETUDE FENCE (261120 — child 5 of the étude embed epic, *Blog - an etude fence on a content page renders the player*). A fenced `etude` block in a content page's markdown — exactly what Multetudes' Settings → Copy note block copies — renders as atetudes.com's own player of that étude, with the block as its #etude= fragment, and a plain link beneath it for a reader that strips iframes (a feed reader). The post keeps the READABLE block; nothing here reads its values or restates a rule. Whether the étude may play is the player's own playable() — checked on the BUILT pages by tools/check_site.py, which fails the build on a bad or custom block, by name. THE ENCODING is etude-link's (engine/etude-link.mjs): the block body percent-encoded after #etude=. urlquery is Go's QueryEscape — every reserved character escaped, a space written "+" — so "+" becomes "%20" (a literal "+" in the block is already "%2B"). The reader is decodeURIComponent, which reads this as it reads encodeURIComponent's output. THE PATH is the block's own `app:` line: /embed// (a published, permanent address — the epic's permanence note). THE HEIGHT is the Multetudes door's measured embed height (hub/doors/multetudes.door.mjs `embed.height`), stated in hugo.yaml `params.etudeEmbedHeight`; check_site.py asserts the two agree. THE FRAME FITS ITS ÉTUDE (261123, *Player - the frame fits the étude, no white space under it*): the player states its height — its tallest chord at the frame's width (hub/modules/etude-player.mjs, message atetudes:player-height, hub/embed-fit.mjs) — and the one script below, written once per page, sets each étude frame to what ITS player said (the message's source must be that frame's window, from this site).
