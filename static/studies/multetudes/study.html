Moving the box. Drag the ■ grip to move it — across frets, and across strings with it. Drag the ● handles on its top and bottom edges to choose which strings it spans. With the neck focused, ← → step the frame one scale note and ↑ ↓ slide the whole set across the strings. Clicking any note on the anchor string seats the frame there.
